GOLDEN NPC 2B-5 — EVE CANONICAL LIFE HISTORY

Architecture
------------
TRUE HISTORY remains WorldEvents in project_eve_history.db.

This patch adds:
1. Birth of Eve and Adam Sinclair
2. Eve rents a room at Adam's house
3. Eve becomes manager at Sinclair Coffee
4. Eve chooses to remain in Bellefontaine after nearly taking an out-of-town job

Also adds:
- EventParticipants
- locked EventFacts
- causal/influence links

It does NOT create:
- PersonalMemories
- KnowledgeItems
- RelationshipReasons

Those subjective records belong in 2B-6 and should point back to these EventIds.

Date policy
-----------
Only Eve's birth date/time is exact.
Other event dates remain blank with DatePrecision='Unknown'.
No fake year/age is invented.

Install
-------
Extract into:
D:\ProjectEve\code\ProjectEve_Clean\ProjectEve_Clean

Run:
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\apply_golden_npc_2B5.ps1"
dotnet build
dotnet run -- golden-populate-life-history
dotnet run -- golden-audit 1

Expected audit change:
World event participation should increase from its current count because
four additional canonical Eve life events are added.

Do not commit until the output is reviewed.
