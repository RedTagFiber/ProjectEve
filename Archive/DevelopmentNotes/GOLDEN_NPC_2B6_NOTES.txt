GOLDEN NPC 2B-6 — EVE SUBJECTIVE LAYER

Purpose
-------
Connect Eve's subjective memories and knowledge to the four canonical
TRUE HISTORY EventIds created in 2B-5.

Rules
-----
- TRUE HISTORY remains objective.
- PersonalMemories and KnowledgeItems live in RELATIONSHIPS.
- Eve does NOT automatically receive every objective fact.
- Adam and Lisa do NOT automatically receive copied records.
- Eve gets no impossible first-person memory of her own birth.
